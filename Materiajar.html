6 to 14. Grammar Focus

WH-QUESTIONS

WH-questions are used to get specific information.

WHAT

Things / Information

What is your name?

→ My name is Andi.

WHERE

Place

Where are you from?

→ I am from Banggai.

WHEN

Time

When is your birthday?

→ It is in July.

WHO

Person

Who is your best friend?

→ My best friend is Rina.

WHY

Reason

Why do you like English?

→ Because it is interesting.

HOW

Manner / Condition

How are you?

→ I'm fine.

⭐ SPECIAL QUESTION: HOW OLD...?

We use How old are you? to ask about someone's age.

❌ I have 15 years old.
✅ I am 15 years old.

21. Personal Information & Digital Safety

Not all personal information should be shared with everyone. Think before you share!

Generally Appropriate

Safe for classroom introductions.

  • Name / Nickname
  • Class & Hometown
  • Hobbies & Interests
  • Dreams

Share Carefully

Only with people you trust.

  • Phone number
  • Detailed home location
  • Personal social-media account

Never Share Publicly

Keep this completely private.

  • Passwords & PINs
  • OTP codes
  • Banking information

Family Members 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Master talking about family, relationships, and possessive adjectives.

1. Lesson Overview

Family is an important part of our lives. In this lesson, students learn how to talk about family members, describe relationships, and tell simple information about their families in English.

Students will learn to use:

my your his her its our their
Essential Understanding: "Every family is unique. Learning how to talk about our family helps us understand ourselves and appreciate other people's families."

2. Learning Objectives

Identify common family members
Understand possessive adjectives functions
Use my, your, his, her, etc. correctly
Ask and answer questions about family
Create a simple family profile

7 to 10. Grammar Focus

POSSESSIVE ADJECTIVES

Possessive adjectives are used to show ownership, relationship, or connection.
Rule: Possessive Adjective + Noun (e.g., my mother).

Subject Pronoun Possessive Adjective Example in a Sentence
ImyMy mother is a teacher.
YouyourWhat is your brother's name?
HehisHis father is a doctor.
SheherHer sister is a student.
ItitsThe cat is with its owner.
WeourOur family is happy.
TheytheirTheir parents are teachers.

DON'T CONFUSE THEM!

HE → HIS (Male) He is my brother.
His name is Andi.
SHE → HER (Female) She is my sister.
Her name is Rina.
THEY → THEIR (Plural) They are my parents.
Their house is in Banggai.

13 & 14. Reading: My Family

My Family

My name is Dika. I am a student of Class X-B at SMA Negeri 1 Banggai. I live with my family in Banggai.

There are five people in my family. My father's name is Budi. He is a teacher. My mother's name is Sari. She is a nurse.

I have one brother and one sister. My brother's name is Rian. He is a student. My sister's name is Dina. She likes reading books.

We love our family very much. We usually have dinner together and talk about our day.

14. Reading Check (Mental Check)

  • 1. Where does Dika live? He lives in Banggai.
  • 2. How many people are in his family? There are five people.
  • 3. What does his mother do? She is a nurse.
  • 15. Deep Thinking: What do 'my' and 'our' tell us? They show a relationship or connection between people and things (possession).

16 & 19. Dialogue & Interview

Talking About Family

A: Do you have a big family?

B: Yes, I do.

A: How many people are in your family?

B: There are five people in my family.

A: Who are they?

B: They are my father, my mother, my brother, my sister, and me.

A: What does your father do?

B: He is a teacher.

Family Interview Card

Use these questions to interview your partner.

  • How many people are in your family?
  • Who are they?
  • Do you have any brothers or sisters?
  • What does your father/mother do?
  • What does your family usually do together?
